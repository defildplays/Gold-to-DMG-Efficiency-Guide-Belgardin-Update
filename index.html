Bracer upgrades, checkpoint by checkpoint

05 · Market inputs

Market prices

Luminous upgrades use the cooldown gem prices below. Level 8 and Level 9 prices are market averages; Level 10 cooldown and damage gems have separate prices. The two T3 damage-gem transitions use separate T3 prices; the current T4 damage-gem price does not apply to them.
Ability Stone average costs
Each attempt costs 21,250g: a 1,000g stone plus 9 Pheons at 2,250g each. With optimal cutting, either engraving on the 9-node line, and at most 4 negative nodes: 9/6 or better has a 0.6546% chance (~153 stones, 3.25m gold); 9/7 or better has a 0.1186% chance (~843 stones, 17.92m gold). These are averages, not guarantees. Free stones and Pheons reduce actual spending. A 9/6 → 9/7 upgrade requires a new stone, so its cost is the full expected 9/7 hunt rather than the difference between the two averages. Damage gains depend on your engraving pair; the 7/7 → 9/6 gain is derived from the two existing stone upgrade multipliers.

Level 6 and 7 retain their existing estimated prices. Level 8 and 9 use the stated average market prices. Upgrade cost is the target gem price minus the starting gem value.

Accessory upgrade costs use the target set price minus the starting accessory value. Express accessories are treated as having no resale offset. Fees and the actual resale value of your pieces can change the difference.
