04 · Market inputs

EU market prices

Luminous gem rows use the 3.6m Level 10 cooldown price. A damage gem ladder uses 3.7m instead. The two T3 damage-gem transitions use separate T3 prices; the current T4 damage-gem price does not apply to them.

Accessory upgrade costs use the target set price minus the starting accessory value. Express accessories are treated as having no resale offset. Fees and the actual resale value of your pieces can change the difference.
